# 📦 Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📖 Project Overview

The Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard is an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed in Power BI to provide comprehensive visibility into supply chain operations. The dashboard integrates inventory, shipment, supplier, and customer data into a centralized analytical platform, enabling stakeholders to monitor performance, identify operational bottlenecks, and support strategic decision-making.

By transforming raw operational data into actionable insights, this solution helps organizations improve efficiency, optimize resource utilization, and enhance overall supply chain performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Organizations often struggle with fragmented supply chain data, making it difficult to evaluate operational performance and respond quickly to changing business conditions.

This dashboard was designed to:

* Monitor key supply chain KPIs in real time
* Improve visibility across inventory and logistics operations
* Evaluate supplier and customer performance
* Identify trends, inefficiencies, and growth opportunities
* Support data-driven decision-making through interactive analytics

---

## 📊 Dashboard Components

### Executive Overview

Provides a high-level snapshot of overall business performance, including:

* Total Revenue
* Total Orders
* Total Shipments
* Revenue Trends
* Operational Performance Summary

### Inventory Analysis

Focused on inventory optimization and stock monitoring:

* Inventory Turnover Analysis
* Stock Level Monitoring
* Product Performance Evaluation
* Inventory Trend Analysis

### Shipment Analysis

Measures logistics efficiency and shipment performance:

* Shipment Volume Tracking
* Shipment Cost Analysis
* Cost Per Shipment
* Delivery Performance Monitoring

### Supplier Analysis

Evaluates supplier contribution and operational effectiveness:

* Supplier Performance Analysis
* Supplier Contribution Metrics
* Order Distribution Insights
* Supplier Comparison Dashboard

### Customer Analysis

Provides insights into customer behavior and revenue generation:

* Customer Order Trends
* Revenue by Customer
* Customer Contribution Analysis
* Customer Performance Insights

### Insights & Recommendations

Highlights key findings and business opportunities:

* Performance Trends
* Operational Bottlenecks
* Strategic Recommendations
* Actionable Business Insights
